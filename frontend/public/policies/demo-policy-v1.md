# SchemeGuard Learning Scholarship — FICTIONAL DEVELOPMENT POLICY

This is an authored software test fixture, not an Indian government scheme.
It offers no money, has no official application process, and must never be used
to claim government eligibility. All thresholds and dates below are invented
for deterministic software testing. Use synthetic profiles only.

Policy version: 1
Authored: 2026-11-12
Effective for this fixture: 2026-01-01 onwards
Approval basis: synthetic development fixture, not government or human policy approval.

## 1. Age
Declared age must be at least 18 and at most 25, inclusive.

## 2. Household income
Declared annual household income must be at most INR 300000, inclusive.

## 3. Study pathway
The applicant must study full time in either engineering or science.
Part-time study and other course categories do not meet this test condition.

## 4. Exclusion
An applicant receiving another scholarship is excluded from this fictional award.

## 5. Conditional support
If disability support is requested, the declared certified disability percentage
must be at least 40, inclusive. If support is not requested, this additional
percentage requirement does not apply. An unknown support request is UNKNOWN.

## 6. Enrollment date
The declared enrollment date must be from 2026-06-01 through 2026-12-31,
inclusive. These dates are test inputs, not government application deadlines.

## 7. Missing information
Missing or null values produce UNKNOWN for comparisons. AND fails if any child
fails, otherwise is unknown if any child is unknown, otherwise passes. OR passes
if any child passes, otherwise is unknown if any child is unknown, otherwise fails.
An OR branch that already passes does not require missing alternative fields.

## 8. Meaning of the assessment
PASS means "Known conditions satisfied". FAIL means "Requirements not satisfied".
UNKNOWN means "More information required". These are software assessments of
this fixture only. They do not constitute official approval or a scholarship award.
